YLP Showcase show

Join us on 1st July from 1:00pm to 4:00pm for a special celebration of the achievements, growth, and progress of our young people over the past year.

This event will bring together young people, tutors, families, partners, and members of the wider community to recognise accomplishments, showcase talents, and share inspiring stories of learning and development. Through performances, displays, and activities, attendees will have the opportunity to see firsthand the incredible work and dedication demonstrated throughout the year.

We warmly invite everyone to come along, celebrate with us, and support our young people as they reflect on their journeys and look ahead to future opportunities.
