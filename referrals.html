Referrals

Most referrals to YLP are received through schools, local authorities, and other agencies working with young people and their families. Young people can also be referred by youth services or other organisations involved in education and personal development.

On receipt of a referral, parents or carers are contacted to arrange a visit to YLP. This visit provides an opportunity for staff to meet the young person, discuss their interests, strengths, and support needs, and ensure the programme is a good fit.

Where a referral has been made by an external organisation, we also contact the referrer to gain a fuller understanding of the young person’s needs and any relevant background information. This collaborative approach helps us put appropriate support in place from the outset.

Onboarding Process

1

Initial Visit

Once a referral is received, we contact parents or carers to arrange a visit where we meet the young person, discuss their interests and needs, and explain the programme.

2

Assessment

We review the young person's goals, skills, and support requirements, liaising with the referrer and other agencies to ensure we fully understand their circumstances.

3

Induction

Young people are invited to attend an induction session covering programme expectations, health and safety, daily routines, and the support available to them.

4

Trial

If the placement is suitable, the young person is offered a trial period to experience the programme before committing to longer-term placement.

5

Start

Following successful completion of the trial period, the young person officially starts the programme and engages with all activities and support offered.
