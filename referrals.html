Referrals

Most referrals to YLP are received through schools, local authorities, and other agencies working with young people and their families. Young people can also be referred by youth services or other organisations involved in education and personal development.

On receipt of a referral, parents or carers are contacted to arrange a visit to YLP. This visit provides an opportunity for staff to meet the young person, discuss their interests, strengths, and support needs, and ensure the programme is a good fit.

Where a referral has been made by an external organisation, we also contact the referrer to gain a fuller understanding of the young person’s needs and any relevant background information. This collaborative approach helps us put appropriate support in place from the outset.
